MAZ Launcher finds your games by itself. Starting, installing and uninstalling always happens through the official clients.

Every game, installed or not

Roblox with your favorites

Paste a game link from roblox.com and it becomes its own tile that starts straight into the game.

Free games with a countdown

See what's free on Epic and Steam right now and how long the offer lasts. One click on "Get" and it's yours.

Errors explained in plain words

If a game won't start or crashes, the launcher explains step by step what happened – with buttons that fix it.

Download

Pick your system. Every file is listed with a checksum in SHA256SUMS.txt.

  • MAZ-Launcher.exe
    Windows 10/11, 64-bit (most PCs)    Download
  • MAZ-Launcher-arm64.exe
    Windows on ARM (e.g. Snapdragon laptops)    Download

Double-click the file and choose "Install". If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC": click "More info", then "Run anyway".

  • MAZ Launcher (.dmg)
    Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 11 or newer    Download

Open the .dmg and drag MAZ Launcher into "Applications". The first time, right-click the app and choose "Open" – macOS asks once because the app isn't from the App Store.

Needs webkit2gtk-4.1 (already installed on most desktops). Epic games run through the Heroic Games Launcher, Roblox through Sober.

Runs on your computer. Only there.

No MAZ account, no tracking, no ads. Your library and settings stay on your computer.

  • Passwords stay with Steam, Epic and Roblox – the launcher never asks for them.
  • Internet is only used for cover art, the free-games list and the update check.
