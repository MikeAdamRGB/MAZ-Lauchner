01 / Every store

Everything in one place

Open Game Launcher finds your games by itself. Starting, installing and uninstalling always happens through the official clients.

01

10 stores in one library

Steam, Epic, GOG, EA app, Ubisoft Connect, Battle.net, Amazon Games, itch.io, Xbox and Roblox – always started through the official app.

02

Every game, installed or not

Your whole Steam and Epic library – install a game with one click, uninstall it just as easily.

03

Minecraft with mods

Mods, resource packs, shaders and data packs from Modrinth and CurseForge – installed with one click. NoRisk Client profiles start straight from your library.

04

Free games with a countdown

See what's free on Epic and Steam right now and how long the offer lasts.

05

Errors explained in plain words

If a game won't start or crashes, the launcher explains step by step what happened – with buttons that fix it.

06

Your look

Three layouts (OG, Steam, Epic), 20 designs, your own designs with a live editor and a background image.

07

Your library, your order

Tags, collections, genres, playtime and filters. Games you own twice show up once. Emulators and ROM folders too.

08

Your own games too

Add any game or emulator. Before every start a short checklist asks if everything important is closed.

09

Updates inside the app

You get a message when a new version is out and update with one click – every download is checked first.

03 / Free download

Download

Pick your system. Every file is listed with a checksum in SHA256SUMS.txt.

  • Open-Game-Launcher.exe
    Windows 10/11, 64-bit (most PCs)    Download
  • Open-Game-Launcher-arm64.exe
    Windows on ARM (e.g. Snapdragon laptops)    Download

Double-click the file and choose "Install". If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC": click "More info", then "Run anyway".

  • Open Game Launcher (.dmg)
    Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 11 or newer    Download

Open the .dmg and drag Open Game Launcher into "Applications". The first time, right-click the app and choose "Open".

  • AppImage
    Any distribution – make it executable and start    Download
  • .deb
    Ubuntu, Debian, Linux Mint, Pop!_OS    Download
  • .rpm
    Fedora, openSUSE, Nobara    Download
  • .pkg.tar.zst
    Arch, Manjaro, EndeavourOS, CachyOS (Pacman)    Download
  • Flatpak
    Sandboxed, any distribution    Download
  • Snap
    Ubuntu & Snap    Download

Needs webkit2gtk-4.1 (already installed on most desktops). Epic games run through the Heroic Games Launcher, Roblox through Sober.

04 / Local & open

Runs on your computer. Only there.

No account, no tracking, no ads. Your library and settings stay on your computer.

A

Open source

B

No passwords

Passwords stay with Steam, Epic and Roblox – the launcher never asks for them.

C

Local first

Internet is only used for cover art, the free-games list and the update check.
